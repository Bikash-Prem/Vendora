# VoiceOps AI

VoiceOps AI is a voice-first business operating system designed for the informal economy.

The platform enables small vendors, shopkeepers, farmers, and local businesses to manage daily business operations using natural voice input in regional languages.

Instead of manually typing transactions or maintaining notebooks, users can simply speak naturally, and the system automatically converts spoken business activity into structured digital records and analytics.

---

# Problem Statement

Millions of small businesses and informal workers still operate without proper digital tools because:

- Most business software assumes literacy and typing ability
- Manual bookkeeping consumes time
- Financial records are unstructured or missing
- Small vendors struggle with inventory tracking and analytics
- Voice interaction in local languages is still underserved

VoiceOps AI aims to remove literacy as a barrier to business digitization.
